RatConTRAPtion

The rat trap, for one or two players.

How to play

A spring bar presses down on the pegs the whole time. Take turns pulling pegs out. Pull as many as you dare, then bank them.

Each pull lets the pack shift a little. If the bar jumps about 2 mm or more in one go, the trap snaps and you lose everything you pulled that turn. Pegs lined up under the bar carry the most force.

1 point 2 points 3 points

The game ends when the trap shuts past the arrows or the last peg is out.
